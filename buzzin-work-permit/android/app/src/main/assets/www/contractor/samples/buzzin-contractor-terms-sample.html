Contractor Terms & Community Guidelines

These terms apply to every contractor, supplier and worker who carries out work inside Buzzin community. Read each section and tick the box to confirm you agree. The community may stop work that does not follow these rules.

1. Working hours

  1. Work is allowed Saturday to Thursday, 08:00–18:00. No work on Fridays or public holidays.
  2. Noisy work (drilling, cutting, hammering) is allowed only 09:00–13:00 and 15:00–17:00.
  3. Work outside these hours needs written approval from the community office.
I have read and agree to section 1.

2. Access, ID and parking

  1. Every worker must carry a valid Emirates ID or passport and show their pass QR code at the gate.
  2. Only vehicles listed on the approved permit may enter. Park only in the visitor or service bays.
  3. Passes are personal and must not be shared.
I have read and agree to section 2.

3. Health and safety

  1. Workers must wear suitable PPE at all times, including safety shoes and high-visibility vests in common areas.
  2. Work at height, hot work and electrical isolation need an approved method statement before starting.
  3. A working fire extinguisher must be available in the work area.
I have read and agree to section 3.

4. Noise, dust and odours

  1. Keep doors closed and use dust sheets to protect neighbouring units and corridors.
  2. Do not cover or disable smoke detectors without approval from the community office.
I have read and agree to section 4.

5. Waste and cleaning

  1. Remove all debris and packaging from the community every day. Do not use residents' bins or chutes.
  2. Clean any common area you use, including lifts, corridors and loading bays.
I have read and agree to section 5.

6. Lifts and common areas

  1. Use only the service lift to move materials. Lift protection must be in place before use.
  2. Do not store materials or tools in corridors, staircases or car parks.
I have read and agree to section 6.

7. Damage and liability

  1. The contractor is responsible for any damage caused by its workers to community property or other units.
  2. The contractor must hold valid insurance for the full duration of the work.
I have read and agree to section 7.

8. Breach of these terms

  1. The community may suspend the permit, refuse entry or ask workers to leave if these terms are not followed.
  2. Repeated breaches can lead to the contractor being blocked from future work in the community.
I have read and agree to section 8.
